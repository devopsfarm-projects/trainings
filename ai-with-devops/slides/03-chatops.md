# Module 03 — ChatOps & Controlled Automation

Duration: 30 min theory / 90 min hands-on (total 120 min)

Slide outline / speaker notes

- Title slide: "ChatOps patterns and safety"
- ChatOps architectures (2 slides): bots, webhooks, event-driven, serverless
- Human-in-the-loop patterns (2 slides): approvals, dry-run, auditable transcripts
- Security considerations (2 slides): ephemeral credentials, scopes, least privilege
- Demo flow (1 slide): Natural language request → proposed plan → approval → action
- Live demo notes: show starter bot that prints manifest diffs instead of applying

Speaker notes
- Stress the importance of dry-run during demos and in classrooms.
- Explain how to design idempotent operations and safe rollbacks.
